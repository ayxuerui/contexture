How to get a second opinion: three AI models from different families review a plan, or answer one question, and you compare what they say. Call `second_opinion_preflight` once to check the critics answer. Call `second_opinion_critique` with the whole plan as `plan`, or `second_opinion_poll` with the question as `prompt`. The models see only that text, so write out everything they need. When a result starts with `running:` and a run id, call `second_opinion_result` with that id until the answers come back. One second opinion runs at a time. When the `ctxr-second-opinion` skill is installed, follow it.
