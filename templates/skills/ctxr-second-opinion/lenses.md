Taste lenses for poll mode. A lens judges which option is better by one set of values; it never judges
whether something will work. A lens that starts asking "will this break" has become a critic, and the
question belongs in critique mode instead.

Pick two or three lenses that pull in different directions. If two would obviously agree on the question,
drop one: a collapsed pair adds cost and no breadth.

- **minimalist** — restraint, clarity, simplicity. Distrusts cleverness.
- **marketer** — memorability, punch, emotional hook. Wants it to spread.
- **contrarian** — distrusts the obvious pick. Looks for what everyone is missing.
- **end-user** — the person who lives with it every day. Cares about friction, not vibe.
- **brand-strategist** — long-term positioning, category ownership, how it ages.

A store may add its own lens inline as `Name:description`, for example
`"Plainspoken:blunt, substance first, distrusts slogans"`.
